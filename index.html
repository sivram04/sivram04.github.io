01About

I translate business requirements into production AI systems, forecasting solutions, and data products that improve how organizations decide.

I'm a Data Analyst working across AI engineering, system design, and analytics, and an M.S. Business Analytics graduate from the University of New Haven. My work runs the full lifecycle — designing data schemas and pipelines, migrating ML systems to the cloud, and turning large operational datasets into decisions leaders can act on.

On the engineering side, I recently led four engineers rebuilding a production AI headshot pipeline on Azure — designing an async queue and a 27-table SQL schema, and A/B-tuning training to make it 46% faster, 32% better at identity preservation, and 40% cheaper.

On the analytics side, I build SQL + Python forecasting engines and BI dashboards in Power BI, Looker Studio, and Tableau — modeling tens of millions of records into forecasts, KPI marts, and executive-level planning, with forecasting work that translated into six-figure inventory savings.

Most recently, I founded Lakshyaa, where I'm building a financial decision platform: a deterministic, explainable engine that helps businesses make better cash-management decisions from their own accounting data. Across all of it, I care about connecting analytical and AI outputs to measurable outcomes.
